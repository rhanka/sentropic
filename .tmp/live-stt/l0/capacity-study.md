## Campaign sizing and daily shares

For each primary tuple A–E: three control-path chains × (one seed + 50 successors) = 153 counted units; add 50 bounded-tool/fault/language/restart/failure units = **203**. Five tuples use **1,015**; adjunct second-engine/mixed-stage checks use **10**; total **1,025**, with **750 measured interruptions**. Deterministic tests consume no native unit. Every failed/restarted chain remains counted; reserve is not permission to exceed the ceiling. Independent review and later provider/platform campaigns need their own allocations.

R3's 103-day figure uses all ten units/day; it is only an unconstrained global-cap lower bound. The source partition makes the practical best cases **513 normal-mode days** or **129 native-campaign days**, before other uses, rolling windows, outages or cloud constraints. No protected enrollment share is available to voice.

Let `P` be the native/maintenance partition (2 or 8), `H_d` higher-priority use, `V_d` separately reserved everyday voice and `O_d` other accepted work. The allocator must bound daily campaign share by `D_d <= max(0, min(P - H_d - V_d - O_d, 10 - all_spent_and_reserved_d))`, plus fresh provider-window admission. Completion requires the sum of actual dated shares to reach 1,025, not simply a configured ceiling. Current evidenced `D_d = 0` because admission evidence is absent.

Dates are inclusive arithmetic examples, **not bookings**. Actual start is no earlier than all native/cloud/ledger/license/release gates and allocator admission. At eight/day the final day uses one campaign unit; at two/day it uses one. Reserving all eight in advance for 129 days is not a valid substitute for fresh daily admission. A 12–15-turn functional walkthrough takes at least 6–8 normal days or two campaign days at these maxima; it earns no n=50/p95 acceptance.

Everyday interactive use must be explicitly classified by allocator authority; pending that classification it is unavailable. If charged to the current native partition, a 30-turn conversation alone needs at least 15 normal days or four campaign days with no other use. Continuous low-latency conversation is therefore not supported by a claim of “ten/day”: backend admission waiting is separate from STT/barge-in latency. A useful dedicated interactive service needs a sustained entitled turn rate plus reserved burst/window headroom, not only one initial session admission.

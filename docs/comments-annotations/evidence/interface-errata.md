## Precedence erratum

Historical E2/TR2 attribution of “sans conservation / no conservation” to the owner is superseded: this was i-cond's reformulation. TREAT2 directly validates the narrow `prospect_notes` / Immo legacy-notes scope and the two exact register quotations in [owner decisions](owner-decisions.md).

Historical Immo backup expiry, new-copy bans and backup anti-resurrection prescriptions are superseded by TREAT2. Immo's backups and lifecycle are outside this SPEC. Count-only conversion evidence remains; Sentropic retains its own backup/restore rehearsal. No per-row exclusion detail may enter manifests, receipts, logs or exports produced with comments tooling, and no quarantine or invented hold may bypass the owner-approved eradication. This imposes no Immo backup requirement.

The interface maintainer must supply this erratum and record host acknowledgement with the P1 freeze receipt before host preparation relies on E2. Delivery and acknowledgement have not occurred in this preparation. E2/TR2 originals remain unchanged.

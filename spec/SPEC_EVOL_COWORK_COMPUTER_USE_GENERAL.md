# SPEC EVOL — General Cowork Computer Use Safety Envelope

> **Status:** DESIGN ONLY; no product code. Every `D*` below is a recommendation pending the non-delegable architect/Opus ratification in §9. No GENERAL build or release may begin until every §9 answer is recorded. “General” means a full browser/desktop surface inside the contained target, not authorization to touch arbitrary networks, production systems, host machines, or real credentials.

## 1. Grounding, scope, and the problem that must be solved

The MVP architect ratified six binding conditions: per-invocation broker closure (C1), human target selection even with one device (C2), device-proof delivery (C3), a server-signed lease plus device-signed acknowledgement (C4), isolated non-admin throwaway VM plus revoke-before-device-delete (C5a/C5b), and the h2a PR #152 publisher citation (C6) (`/home/antoinefa/src/sentropic/tmp/COWORK_MVP_RATIFICATION_archi.md:10-28,30-31`). The resulting MVP is deliberately Notepad-only, uses synthetic text, permits no Enter, and reports ambiguous execution as PAS-FAIT (`spec/SPEC_EVOL_COWORK_CONNECTOR_HOST_MVP.md:43-53,65-80`).
That MVP could make semantic dangers unreachable by locking the VM to one benign app. It did not make semantic action policy authoritative: `DesktopCapabilityProvider` exposes raw screen capture, absolute click, literal type, scroll, and key operations (`packages/cowork-desktop/src/capability/types.ts:51-71`); `input_action` validates model-originated coordinates/text/combo and dispatches them directly (`packages/cowork-desktop/src/tools/input-action.ts:59-105`); current consent is tool-and-origin-wide and may persist `allow_always` (`packages/cowork-desktop/src/consent/manager.ts:13-28,48-57,65-98`). A click cannot itself prove whether it means “Buy”, “Delete”, or “Share”; the verified blocker therefore rejects keyword filters and model-supplied risk classes as non-authoritative and identifies trusted native attestation or safety-by-construction as the honest forks (`/home/antoinefa/src/sentropic/tmp/COWORK_MVP_F3_BLOCKER_cowork.md:7-22`). GENERAL removes the benign-environment crutch, so this semantic gap is the core design problem, not a later hardening item.
The threat baseline is four boundary crossings—model to native input, pixels to model, hostile content to policy, and distribution to workstation—and includes injection, confused deputy, wrong device, credential theft, overcapture, privilege amplification, replay, and unreviewable incidents (`spec/SPEC_STUDY_COWORK_COMPUTER_USE_BENCHMARK.md:151-177`, source commit `d351783d8`). Its release gates require device binding, two-sided authorization, action-aware consent, no permanent hands, immediate sensitive-action confirmation, isolation, egress defenses, device-bound identity, signed distribution, audit/kill switch, and invariant tests (`spec/SPEC_STUDY_COWORK_COMPUTER_USE_BENCHMARK.md:179-203`).
Reuse, do not redesign, the existing foundation: durable Ed25519 device identity and active-owner lookup (`api/src/services/cowork/device-identity.ts:7-25,27-59,67-83`), durable presence (`api/src/services/cowork/device-registry.ts:10-23,26-72`), transactional freshness/ownership-gated lease issuance and device-signed ack (`api/src/services/cowork/device-lease-service.ts:32-111,114-150`), and the device/presence/lease tables (`api/drizzle/0041_cowork_device_identity_lease.sql:4-47`). The current lease return shape contains no server signature and the ack covers only `leaseId.nonce` (`api/src/services/cowork/device-lease-service.ts:14-25,131-149`), so C4 remains a mandatory GENERAL strengthening, not an already-implemented guarantee. Reuse the connector-host’s deny-by-default principal/workspace/exposure intersection (`packages/connector-host/src/mount.ts:181-257`) and invoke seam (`packages/connector-host/src/mount.ts:260-286`), but keep C1’s fresh per-invocation broker closure; shared mount state is expressly rejected as a cross-invocation race (`/home/antoinefa/src/sentropic/tmp/COWORK_MVP_RATIFICATION_archi.md:10-10`).

## 2. Authoritative action safety

| Candidate | What could be authoritative | Residual risk / disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **A — attestation only** | A trusted, protected device policy-enforcement point (PEP) measures foreground process/window and OS UI element; a managed-browser bridge may additionally measure origin/tab/DOM role. The broker accepts only a PEP-signed verdict derived from a versioned positive allowlist. | UI Automation is absent or misleading for canvas, remote, legacy, and adversarial apps; web/app code can supply deceptive accessibility metadata; foreground and element can change between check and input. Fail-closed A would make much of a general surface unusable. **Rejected as the sole authority.** |
| **B — human confirmation for every unproven action** | A human decision made in trusted controller chrome or a protected local supervisor, outside the model-controlled surface, authorizes the exact immutable action. | Confirmation fatigue, misunderstanding, and social engineering remain; a human can approve harm. It is safe only if the UI cannot be spoofed and the action is rebound/rechecked immediately before execution. **Required fallback, not the only mode.** |
| **C — hybrid** | PEP-attested, positive-allowlisted low-risk actions may proceed under a narrow prior human scope; every absent, stale, conflicting, unsupported, or non-low-risk proof requires exact per-action human confirmation. | A PEP/policy bug can falsely prove low risk; human fallback retains B’s risks. Endpoint integrity, short proof lifetime, same-element recheck, budgets, and adversarial testing reduce but do not eliminate them. **D1 — ratifiable default.** |

**D2 — authority rule.** A model-supplied intent, element name, destination, reversibility claim, or risk label is always `ASSERTED_UNTRUSTED`: it may be shown to the human but can never select a policy branch, mint consent, weaken a scope, or satisfy an attestation field. The machine path is a positive proof of known-low-risk behavior, never a model-fed denylist of bad behavior. Anything not proven low risk is unproven and goes to the human; if the human authority is unavailable, it is PAS-FAIT.

**D3 — trusted action pipeline.** (1) Trusted chat context creates an immutable `ActionDescriptor` inside the C1 broker closure; model arguments are retained as untrusted payload. (2) The target PEP measures code-signed app/package/process identity, integrity/elevation, interactive desktop, foreground window, UIA runtime id/role/pattern/bounds/secure-field flag, and—only through a managed browser authority—origin/tab/form destination. (3) The PEP rejects the secure desktop, elevated targets, stale evidence, unknown apps/roles, proof-provider disagreement, and coordinates outside the measured element. (4) A versioned server policy positively matches an exact low-risk tuple; names/text inferred from pixels are not proof. (5) Immediately before input, the PEP reacquires app/window/element, compares `surfaceEpoch` and bounds, checks the server `killEpoch`, and performs the input itself so the raw executor cannot bypass the gate. (6) Any focus/layout/element change invalidates both machine verdict and prior human receipt and requires a new proof/confirmation. There is no atomic OS transaction spanning UIA and input, so last-instant target substitution remains a named residual risk and all consequential actions stay human-gated.

## 3. Consent, scopes, and sensitive classes

**D4 — deny by default and no session blanket.** A session is only a lifecycle container. Actuation requires either (a) a consumed one-use action receipt or (b) a still-valid narrow scope plus a fresh `LOW_RISK_PROVEN` verdict. Observation defaults to exact window/region capture; whole-screen capture and any newly visible app require confirmation. Permanent unrestricted `input_action`, “allow all”, model-created grants, and silent consent renewal are forbidden.

| Grant | Permitted use | Binding and expiry |
|---|---|---|
| **Per action** | Required for every unknown or sensitive action; default for all actuation without a qualifying scope. | One `actionId`, descriptor version, device/surface, capability/action kind, encrypted-argument digest, app/window/element proof, destination-policy id, reversibility class, policy version, `surfaceEpoch`, `killEpoch`, one-use nonce, and short expiry. Any change or retry needs a new receipt. |
| **Per scope** | Optional only for enumerated positive low-risk tuples such as scroll/navigation or reversible editing in explicitly approved synthetic apps/destinations. | Human-created scope id; exact device, app signer/package/origin, roles/actions, data class, egress rule ids, TTL, action/byte budget, and policy version. Every action derives and consumes a one-use child authorization. It cannot include a sensitive class. |
| **Per session** | Carries target selection, stop state, budgets, and pending-call list. | Never grants an action by itself; session restart, device change, snapshot restore, policy change, or stop invalidates all children. |

**D5 — always-confirm classes.** Credentials/password/2FA, purchase/financial commitment, delete, share, submit (including every Enter/key action), permission change, legal acceptance, upload, download, messaging/send/publish, external mutation, and any irreversible or semantically uncertain action require explicit human confirmation immediately before that exact action even when app/element attestation succeeds. Confirmation is necessary, not sufficient: server policy, containment, lease, device proof, freshness, budget, and egress must also allow it. There is no batch confirmation for these classes. Unrecognized controls are treated as uncertain, not low risk.

Credentials never enter model context or normal `type(text)`: after confirming the exact target, the human or an OS credential broker performs a protected, destination-bound fill while capture is masked. If the PEP cannot authoritatively identify a secure field and destination, automation cannot safely handle the credential and requires direct human takeover; this is an explicit HITL dependency. The trusted confirmation UI displays the actual consequential values needed for an informed decision, but the audit stores only opaque ids and decision metadata.

## 4. Authorization, delivery, execution, and durable outcomes

**D6 — signed action lease v2.** The existing server signing-key discipline signs canonical `{version,kid,leaseId,invocationId,toolCallId,durableCallRef?,principalId,tenantId,workspaceId,targetDeviceId,targetPepKeyId,capability,actionKind,actionDescriptorId,argumentDigest,requiredAuthority,scopeOrReceiptId,attestationProfileId,policyVersion,actionBudget,killEpoch,issuedAt,expiresAt,nonce}`. Raw text, pixels, secrets, URLs, and window titles are not in the envelope. The device verifies signature/key status, exact target, expiry, nonce, policy/kill epochs, and local descriptor digest before acknowledging. General ack v2 signs the complete server-envelope digest plus device id, PEP key id, local `surfaceEpoch`, and local `killEpoch`; the broker verifies it and atomically transitions once. The old `cowork-lease-ack-v1:${leaseId}.${nonce}` proof remains valid only for the MVP (`api/src/services/cowork/device-lease-service.ts:131-149`). Server signature + device ack are two-sided authorization; neither is human consent or proof of execution.

**D7 — device-proof delivery and invocation isolation.** Poll, SSE, wake, ack, result, and stop-status calls require proof by the selected active device/PEP key in addition to bearer ownership; signed records for sibling devices are never returned. The per-invocation broker closure owns descriptor, lease, consent, deadline, result idempotency, and cancellation. Shared mounts contain only durable connector identity/exposure. Advisory hints and model fields may narrow but never select device, scope, authority, or policy; the human still selects the durable target even when only one is eligible, as C2 requires (`/home/antoinefa/src/sentropic/tmp/COWORK_MVP_RATIFICATION_archi.md:16-22`).

**D8 — authoritative execution gate.** The PEP executes only if one transactionally consistent decision contains: valid server lease; valid device ack; matching unconsumed exact receipt **or** valid low-risk scope plus fresh PEP verdict; unchanged descriptor/proof/policy/kill epochs; active device; and atomic `ready -> executing`. Result acceptance is idempotent and bound to the same tuple. A device assertion that input was emitted is not proof that the intended external effect occurred.

**D9 — honest async state.** A durable `ActionCall` is the source of truth. `DÉPOSÉ-EN-ATTENTE` is returned only after an idempotent `DurableCallRef` and encrypted descriptor are committed, visible in the human pending queue, but **before** any execution lease exists; deposit is not consent and not execution. Wake/reconnect revalidates target, policy, stop epoch, expiry, scope, and attestation, then issues a fresh lease. Sensitive/unknown actions always require a fresh exact confirmation after wake. `FAIT` requires accepted terminal result plus declared postcondition evidence; `PAS-FAIT` covers deny, stop, expiry, mismatch, proof/attestation failure, ambiguous effect, or cancellation. A timeout may remain DÉPOSÉ only while the durable record is honestly pending; otherwise it becomes PAS-FAIT. No expired lease can execute later.

## 5. Kill switch, containment, audit, and reversibility

**D10 — latched global STOP.** Human controller chrome exposes an always-visible **STOP ALL COWORK** control outside the remote framebuffer, consent modal, and model. A separate protected target-supervisor hotkey/control works without network; a hypervisor management-plane stop is the operator fallback. Server STOP atomically increments a monotonic user/workspace `killEpoch`, blocks new issuance, cancels every pending `ActionCall`, revokes all issued/acknowledged leases, and sends a priority stop. Local STOP first latches the PEP closed, releases held keys/buttons, stops input and capture, clears transient pixel/argument buffers, then reports upstream. Every pre-input check compares the epoch. Re-arm requires a fresh human act and new session/scopes; neither model nor reconnect can re-arm. Server-path latency remains a residual risk, which is why local and hypervisor paths must be independent of the guest surface.

**D11 — containment baseline.** GENERAL remains an isolated, non-admin, disposable VM, never the host, developer machine, production cluster, or real-infrastructure jump host (C5a; `/home/antoinefa/src/sentropic/tmp/COWORK_MVP_RATIFICATION_archi.md:26-26`). It has no host mounts, shared clipboard, drive/device forwarding, inbound service, cloud metadata route, control-plane credential, production DNS/route, or resident real credential. Task identities are synthetic or short-lived staging-only, least-privilege, destination/audience-bound, and revoked at teardown; a full surface does not imply full egress.
Network is default-deny through an external egress proxy: allowlisted opaque destination/rule ids only; block RFC1918/link-local/metadata/control-plane ranges, raw IP, unapproved DNS/DoH/QUIC, redirects to denied destinations, and DNS rebinding; uploads/downloads cross a quarantined broker and always use D5 confirmation. The proxy and hypervisor are outside the guest trust boundary. An immutable signed golden image creates a fresh clone per session; device/PEP identity is injected after clone so snapshots cannot clone keys. Checkpoints precede approved high-risk local changes; session end/STOP cuts egress, revokes task identity, quarantines exports, destroys the clone, and restores the golden state. Snapshot rollback cannot undo a sent message, purchase, permission change, upload, or other external effect.

**D12 — ids-only audit.** Append-only, tamper-evident events record only opaque `eventId/sessionId/actionId/actionVersionId/DurableCallRef/invocationId/toolCallId/deviceId/pepKeyId/leaseId/scopeOrReceiptId/attestationEvidenceId/policyId/egressRuleId/snapshotId`, timestamps, state/decision/reason enums, budgets/counts, authority path, and previous-event id/hash. Never record pixels, screenshots, OCR, typed text, arguments, clipboard/files, secrets/tokens, URL/domain/window/element labels, raw attestation properties, or content-derived hashes. Exact descriptors/arguments are separately encrypted, access-controlled, TTL-bound execution state—not audit—and are erased at teardown unless a separately ratified incident hold applies.

Operator review reconstructs the ID-linked timeline and distinguishes proposed, deposited, leased, acknowledged, consented, attested, executing, FAIT, and PAS-FAIT without replaying input. Undo is a new human-authorized workflow: STOP; revoke leases/task identities/scopes; restore/destroy VM; quarantine outputs; then run an explicit compensating action where the external system supports one. No UI or audit may label rollback/undo complete until the external postcondition is measured; irreversible effects remain irreversible.

**D13 — #492 device teardown.** Current device presence and leases cascade from the device row (`api/drizzle/0041_cowork_device_identity_lease.sql:19-40`), so hard deletion would erase live authorization evidence. In one locked transaction: mark device `revoking`; latch/increment its kill epoch; cancel durable calls; revoke every non-terminal lease; revoke scopes/receipts and task credentials; persist an ids-only teardown tombstone/outbox event; then mark `revoked`. Hard delete/cascade is a later retention job permitted only after no non-terminal authorization remains and the tombstone is durable. Any user/workspace cascade must first capture device ids and execute this path. The repository’s #492 pattern explains why cascade runs no application revocation code and why capture/tombstone ordering is load-bearing (`api/src/services/connector-grant-teardown.ts:1-38,81-116,135-176`); for local device leases, revocation occurs before cascade, as C5b explicitly requires (`/home/antoinefa/src/sentropic/tmp/COWORK_MVP_RATIFICATION_archi.md:26-26`).

## 6. `surface/I1`–`surface/I5` from h2a PR #152

The sole publisher defines durable boundary identity, measured-versus-trusted claims, HOLDER/VIEW, existing capability vocabulary, and honest done/deposited/not-done outcomes (`docs/governance/surface-invariants.md:63-81`, **h2a repository, PR #152**). The publisher itself says these were lane-agreed rather than owner-ratified and are not structurally enforced (`docs/governance/surface-invariants.md:23-31,137-140`, h2a PR #152); they are binding here because the Cowork MVP architect explicitly confirmed them and required the publisher citation (`/home/antoinefa/src/sentropic/tmp/COWORK_MVP_RATIFICATION_archi.md:5-6,28-31`).

- **`surface/I1`:** durable `deviceId`/connector instance crosses the boundary; conversation/session ids never route execution. Snapshot-restored clones receive new device/PEP identity.
- **`surface/I2`:** server receipt time, DB transition, trusted-UI click, and PEP OS observation are `MEASURED` by their named holder; model intent/risk, app-provided accessibility names, device-reported input, and external-effect claims are `ASSERTED`. Policy verdict is a decision over cited measurements, not proof of business semantics. Every event carries source, observation time, and freshness.
- **`surface/I3`:** server is HOLDER of durable calls/leases/global stop; controller authority is HOLDER of human receipts; target PEP is HOLDER of local attestation/execution facts; hypervisor/proxy hold containment facts. Model, chat UI, remote framebuffer, and operator projection are VIEW. Conflict, stale holder, or two claimed holders fails closed.
- **`surface/I4`:** public capabilities remain the existing `screen_capture` and `input_action` registry vocabulary (`packages/cowork-desktop/src/tools/registry.ts:12-21`). Action descriptors/attestation are internal authorization data, not a second public tool grammar; unknown capability/action values are dropped.
- **`surface/I5`:** D9 makes FAIT / DÉPOSÉ-EN-ATTENTE / PAS-FAIT durable and mutually exclusive. Ack, consent, deposit, input emission, and model narration are never FAIT by themselves.

## 7. Acceptance invariants and explicit residual boundary

Release must mechanically prove: no raw executor path bypasses the PEP; no model field selects authority; unknown/stale proof cannot auto-run; sensitive classes always consume a fresh human receipt; descriptor changes invalidate receipts; no sibling/cross-tenant/cross-workspace device reads or executes a call; signatures, nonces, expiry, epochs, and one-use transitions reject forgery/replay; STOP prevents later input and capture; revoked devices lose all pending authority before cascade; DÉPOSÉ cannot masquerade as FAIT; and Cowork audit contains no prohibited content.

This envelope cannot make a human infallible, make arbitrary UI metadata truthful, eliminate the UIA-to-input race, prove an external business effect solely from device telemetry, undo inherently irreversible external effects, or guarantee containment after a hypervisor/kernel escape. It therefore prohibits production/real-credential claims, requires HITL wherever positive machine proof is absent, and treats independent adversarial qualification as a release gate rather than evidence that residual risk is zero.

## 11. Scaled adversarial-review plan

Use four independent legs—protocol/tenancy, endpoint/attestation, containment/egress, and human-factors/operations—with reviewers who did not implement the attacked lot. Each critical class gets at least two independent reviewers, deterministic fixtures plus repeated race/fault runs, evidence by opaque ids, and a fresh-leg rerun after remediation. Any unauthorized act, missed sensitive confirmation, cross-device execution, secret/content audit leak, late post-STOP input, sandbox path to host/prod, or false FAIT is an automatic release failure.

| Attack class | Required attacks and pass condition |
|---|---|
| **Consent bypass** | Replay/alter receipts; widen scope; hide Enter in combo/paste/IME; change element/amount/destination after prompt; spoof controller UI; exhaust/fatigue human. No changed or sensitive action executes without a fresh exact receipt. |
| **Attestation spoof / TOCTOU** | Fake UIA roles/names; overlay/window/focus swap; stale runtime id/bounds; canvas/cross-origin iframe; browser/OS provider disagreement; elevated/secure desktop; compromised app. Only positive supported proof auto-runs; every ambiguity gates or stops. |
| **Lease forgery/replay** | Wrong `kid`/signature/device/PEP/tenant/policy/epoch; nonce replay; expired/duplicate/reordered delivery/result; cloned snapshot key; stolen bearer. No unauthorized transition or sibling disclosure; one logical action executes at most once. |
| **Kill-switch defeat** | Drop/delay/flood network; suppress guest notification; stale epoch; stop during key-down/capture/execution/deposit; reconnect/re-arm attempt; guest crash; controller loss. Local latch halts immediately, server revokes all, old work never resumes, hypervisor path remains available. |
| **Sandbox escape / egress** | Host mount/clipboard/device/RDP forwarding, private/link-local/metadata/control-plane access, raw IP, DNS rebinding, redirect, DoH/QUIC, tunnel, malicious download, upload of decoy secret, privilege escalation, VM escape exercise. No path to host/prod/real credential; transfer quarantines hold. |
| **Audit evasion/leak** | Crash between input/result/event, truncate/reorder/fork log, forged device result, teardown cascade, malicious labels/ids, retention race, forbidden pixels/text/URL/hash/token fields. Timeline detects gaps/tampering and contains only the D12 allowlist. |
| **Cross-invocation/tenant leak** | Parallel calls, shared mount/cache, same `toolCallId`, retry, session switch, device reselection, workspace/user deletion, late result. Each closure/ref remains isolated and unauthorized capability stays deny-as-missing. |
| **Async stale wake / false outcome** | Deposit then policy/scope/device/snapshot/identity changes; cancel/STOP/expiry; device wakes after deadline; ambiguous external completion; duplicate compensation. Fresh authority is mandatory; outcome remains honestly DÉPOSÉ or PAS-FAIT, never false FAIT. |
| **Prompt injection / data exfiltration** | Visible/hidden/canvas instructions, poisoned accessibility text, fake consent language, honeytokens, unrelated-window secret, malicious upload/download/message. Model claims never alter authority; egress/credential/capture controls and HITL stop boundary crossing. |
| **Human and recovery failure** | Misleading labels, rapid prompt sequences, inaccessible STOP, operator rollback during outage, compensation without proof, two operators racing. UI preserves exact action/target/consequence, STOP dominates, and no undo is declared without measured postcondition. |

The review dossier must map every finding to §7, a §9 decision, owner, severity, exploit trace, fix, and rerun evidence. The architect receives the complete reconciled dossier—not reviewer deltas—and ratifies residual risk without delegating the GENERAL release decision.
